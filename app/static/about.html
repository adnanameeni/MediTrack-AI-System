About MediTrack AI

MediTrack AI is designed to help users record and monitor blood glucose and blood pressure readings. It provides visual charts, report generation, and an AI assistant that can help summarize recorded information.

MediTrack AI is a tracking and informational tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional medical advice.
